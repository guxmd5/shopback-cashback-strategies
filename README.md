## How Cashback Actually Flows: Pending, Confirmed, Withdrawable

Understanding the three-stage cashback lifecycle prevents a lot of confusion. Here's what happens after you click through and complete a purchase.

**Stage 1: Pending.** Within 48 hours of checkout, your order appears in your ShopBack activity as Pending. The tracking signal has been received — ShopBack knows the sale came from your click-through. The cashback amount is visible but not yet yours to withdraw.

**Stage 2: Confirmed.** Once the merchant validates the order, the cashback moves to Confirmed. For retail purchases, this typically takes a few weeks (it aligns with the store's return window, so a refund doesn't reverse cashback already paid out). For travel bookings, confirmation usually happens after the trip is completed.

**Stage 3: Withdrawable.** Once your Confirmed Cashback reaches $5, you can withdraw it. ShopBack US pays out via PayPal or ACH transfer. The daily withdrawal limit is $300, and there are no withdrawal fees. Typical withdrawal processing takes up to 10 calendar days, though some cases can stretch to 30.

The $5 minimum is deliberately low — it's designed so you can cash out after just a handful of everyday purchases rather than waiting months to accumulate a large balance.
